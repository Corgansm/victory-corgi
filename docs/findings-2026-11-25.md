# Test findings, 25 November 2026

Notes from running `main` at `bafb671` on Windows 11, Intel i7-14700K, NVIDIA RTX 4070 SUPER (driver 621.01),
Series X profile unless said. These are observations from a few runs, judged by eye and from the frame line; treat
them as leads, not proof.

## Lighting

Areas out of the sun now show detail. An earlier fxceless-based build drew them black on the same PC. Some corners
are still close to black.

## Missing walls and floors

- With default settings, parts of several interiors were not drawn (sky visible where walls belong, and parts of
  some characters missing). A build based on fxceless `4e17478` drew the walls of the corridor we compared.
- With all of these set to `0`, two runs showed solid walls in the places captured:
  `VICTORY_MIRROR_FAST`, `VICTORY_FALLBACK_TEXTURES`, `VICTORY_INDIRECT_WINDOW`, `VICTORY_SYSTEM_TEXTURES`,
  `VICTORY_TEXTURE_ALIASES`, `VICTORY_GROUP_SPLIT`, `VICTORY_LDS_UNIFORM`, `VICTORY_PAIR_SYNC`,
  `VICTORY_WATCH_VIEWS`, `VICTORY_PLANE_AHEAD`, `VICTORY_RELAY_COPY_QUEUE`.
- Switching off only part of that list still left geometry missing:
  - `INDIRECT_WINDOW` alone
  - `MIRROR_FAST`, `WATCH_VIEWS`, `PLANE_AHEAD`, `RELAY_COPY_QUEUE`
  - `GROUP_SPLIT`, `LDS_UNIFORM`, `PAIR_SYNC` (with an empty shader cache)
  - `FALLBACK_TEXTURES`, `SYSTEM_TEXTURES`, `TEXTURE_ALIASES`
- No single cause isolated. It may be a combination. Runs did not stop at exactly the same moment, so the
  comparison is not strictly controlled.

## Speed (in the first interiors)

| Settings | Frame rate |
|---|---|
| `VICTORY_RELAY=2` | about 4 fps (about 232 ms a frame, 124 ms of it CPU preparation) |
| plus `VICTORY_LATE_WAIT=3`, `VICTORY_WIDE_BACK=1`, `VICTORY_CARD_BUFFERS_MIN_MB=1`, `VICTORY_CARD_BUFFERS_MB=1024` | about 6 fps, briefly 8 (about 105 ms CPU) |

CPU preparation alone keeps it under about 10 fps with these settings.

## Series S

`--console series-s` with the speed settings above lost the GPU device (driver reported an invalid call) after
about two minutes. Not retried without the speed settings.
